Letter 226-J and the filing forms

Letter 226-J is the IRS letter that proposes an employer shared responsibility payment. It is based on Forms 1094-C and 1095-C and on premium tax credits the IRS allowed. The response date is generally 30 days from the date of the letter. If the employer responds, the IRS replies with a Letter 227. If there is no response, the IRS can assess the payment and send Notice CP 220J.

An applicable large employer files Form 1094-C with the IRS and furnishes Form 1095-C for full-time employees. Those are information returns. The IRS lists a separate penalty for not filing a correct return and for not furnishing a correct statement. For returns and statements due in 2027, the general penalty is $340 per return or statement. It is $60 if corrected within 30 days, and $130 if corrected after 30 days but by August 1. Intentional disregard is $690 per return or statement, with no annual maximum. The same $340, $60, and $130 amounts apply to returns due in 2026, and intentional disregard for that year is $680. Reasonable cause can remove a penalty. Filing with the IRS and furnishing the employee statement are separate.
