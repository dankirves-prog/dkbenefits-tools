## Intro and FAQ

The words below are the indexable page copy.

Employers in Florida and Georgia can use this free calculator to see whether the ACA employer mandate applies, and what a 2026 penalty could look like. DK Benefits made it for owners who want a plain-English estimate first.

An applicable large employer generally averaged at least 50 full-time employees in the prior year, counting full-time equivalents. Full-time means 30 or more hours a week, or 130 hours in a month. Part-time hours are added up, with no person counted above 120 hours, then divided by 120. The year's average rounds down. Seasonal workers can keep a company under the line when the workforce is over 50 for 120 days or fewer. Related companies are counted together.

Section 4980H has two payments, and they are not added together. The A penalty can apply when minimum essential coverage is not offered to enough full-time employees and their dependents, and at least one full-time employee receives a premium tax credit. For 2026 it is $3,340 a year after a 30-employee reduction. The B penalty can apply when coverage is offered but is unaffordable or is not minimum value. For 2026 it is $5,010 a year for each full-time employee who gets a credit, and it cannot exceed the A penalty.

Affordability in 2026 is 9.96 percent. Employers usually use a Form W-2, rate-of-pay, or federal-poverty-line safe harbor. The test uses the lowest-cost self-only premium, not the family premium. For a calendar-year plan in the 48 states and DC, the poverty-line limit is $129.89 a month.

This is an estimate, not legal or tax advice. DK Benefits is a licensed insurance agency, not a tax advisor. Under 50, the mandate does not apply, and small-group coverage may still be worth a look. At 50 or above, review the offer, the self-only premium, and Forms 1094-C and 1095-C.

### Frequently asked questions

**Who has to worry about the ACA employer penalty?**
A company that averaged 50 or more full-time employees, including full-time equivalents, in the prior year. Related companies in a controlled group are counted together. The penalty still depends on whether a full-time employee received a premium tax credit.

**What is the 2026 A penalty?**
It is $3,340 a year, or about $278.33 a month, for each full-time employee after subtracting up to 30. It can apply when coverage is not offered to at least 95 percent of full-time employees and their dependents, or to all but five when five is more.

**What is the 2026 B penalty?**
It is $5,010 a year for each full-time employee who receives a premium tax credit because the offer was unaffordable or did not provide minimum value. It is capped at the A penalty amount.

**How do I know if coverage is affordable in 2026?**
Compare the lowest-cost self-only premium with 9.96 percent of Form W-2 Box 1 wages, the rate of pay, or the federal poverty line. The poverty-line monthly limit for a calendar-year plan in the 48 states and DC is $129.89.
