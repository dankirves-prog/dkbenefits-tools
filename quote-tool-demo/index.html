Why these are starting rates, not final rates

These examples are designed to help you compare directions quickly. Final rates can shift based on participation, census details, underwriting, eligibility, and plan setup timing.

Networks are not insurers

Network labels like PHCS or Cigna describe provider access. A plan can utilize a network while being offered through a different carrier/program structure.

Major medical vs. limited benefit vs. MEC

Major medical options generally provide broader coverage. Limited benefit and MEC options may help in specific budgets or participation situations, but have important limits and caps.

Broader market review

Beyond these starting lanes, a full review can compare fully insured, level-funded, self-funded, and other strategy options based on your goals and workforce profile.

Benefits support beyond rates

Support can include enrollment platforms, new hire and life event support, employee communication, ACA guidance, and general compliance best practices. Employers remain responsible for their own plan compliance, but experienced guidance can make the process much easier.
